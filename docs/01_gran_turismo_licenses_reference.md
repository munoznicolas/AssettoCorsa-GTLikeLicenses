# Gran Turismo License Tests Reference Guide (GT2, GT4, GT5)

## 1. Executive Summary & Educational Philosophy

The **License Test system** in the _Gran Turismo_ series (developed by Polyphony Digital) is widely regarded as one of the most effective driver-education frameworks in gaming history. Rather than throwing players directly into competitive motorsport, the license system breaks high-speed vehicle control down into discrete, fundamental physics principles and incrementally builds driver competence.

### 1.1 The Pedagogical Curve

The curriculum follows a proven pedagogical progression:

1. **Kinematics & Longitudinal Control (B License)**: Straight-line acceleration, threshold braking, stopping distance perception, weight transfer under braking, and single isolated corners.
2. **Directional Control & Combined Dynamics (A License)**: Sequential corners, chicanes, hairpins, trail braking, apex clipping, out-in-out trajectory, and drivetrain behaviors (FF, FR, 4WD, MR).
3. **High-Speed Commitment & Technical Complexes (IB / IC License)**: Medium-to-high speed corners, elevation changes, aerodynamic downforce, off-camber surfaces, and sector chaining.
4. **Racecraft & Precision Control (IA License)**: High-performance machinery (GT500, Group C, LMP, Formula), slipstreaming, overtaking under braking without contact, and blind crests.
5. **Circuit Mastery (Super License / S)**: Full hot laps under zero-tolerance conditions on punishing real-world and fantasy tracks (Nürburgring Nordschleife, Suzuka, Circuit de la Sarthe, Laguna Seca).

## 2. Universal GT License Mechanics & Rules

Across all classic titles (GT2, GT4, GT5), the license tests adhere to a strict set of simulation rules and failure criteria.

### 2.1 Test Archetypes

| Test Type                                   | Description                                                                                                    | Primary Skill Tested                                                      | Typical Duration |
| :------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :--------------- |
| **Acceleration & Braking (Stop Box)**       | Accelerate from 0 to 400m/1000m, then brake into a marked grid box without overshooting or stopping short.     | Threshold braking, braking point visual cues, stopping distance judgment. | 15s – 35s        |
| **Single Corner Sector**                    | Rolling or standing start, navigate one corner (hairpin, sweeper, or 90° turn) and reach the finish line.      | Corner entry speed, apex clipping, throttle application on exit.          | 10s – 25s        |
| **Complex / Chicane Sector**                | Navigate 2 to 4 linked corners (S-curves, chicanes, double apexes).                                            | Transient weight transfer, steering rhythm, curb management.              | 20s – 45s        |
| **Slalom / Cone Course**                    | Weave through cones at speed on an open tarmac area without knocking cones.                                    | Rapid weight shifting, throttle modulation, steering smoothness.          | 20s – 40s        |
| **Pace Car / Follow the Leader**            | Follow an AI pace car through a challenging sector without passing it or colliding with it.                    | Maintaining race pace, visual tracking, drafting distance.                | 45s – 120s       |
| **Overtaking Challenge**                    | Start behind 1 or more AI cars on a sector or full lap; overtake all opponents cleanly before the finish line. | Racecraft, draft/slipstream timing, braking zone passes.                  | 60s – 180s       |
| **Full Circuit Time Trial (Super License)** | A complete, unassisted hot lap of an entire circuit.                                                           | Concentration, consistency, track rhythm, endurance.                      | 60s – 450s       |

### 2.2 Disqualification & Failure Conditions

In Gran Turismo, failing any of the following conditions triggers an immediate **"DISQUALIFIED"** banner, an iconic fail sound effect, and halts the run:

1. **Off-Track Violation**:
   - GT2: Exceeding track boundary limits onto grass/gravel.
   - GT4/GT5: All 4 wheels leaving the paved track surface or white boundary lines. Touching dirt or sand with two or more wheels in severe areas causes instant failure.
2. **Wall / Guardrail Contact**:
   - Any collision with hard scenery (armco barriers, concrete walls, tire stacks) above a minimal contact threshold. Scraping walls to gain time is strictly forbidden.
3. **Vehicle Spin / Loss of Control**:
   - Spinning out (yaw angle exceeding ~90° relative to track trajectory) or sliding backwards across the surface.
4. **Pace Car / AI Opponent Contact**:
   - Touching the AI vehicle or passing before permitted triggers instant disqualification.
5. **Time Expiration (Bronze Target Exceeded)**:
   - If the clock exceeds the Bronze medal threshold, the test ends in failure.
6. **Stop Box Overshoot / Undershoot**:
   - In Acceleration & Braking tests, the car must come to a complete stop ($0\text{ km/h}$) strictly inside the painted white/yellow box. Stopping before the line or skidding past the boundary fails the test.

### 2.3 Reward Tiers (Medals)

- **Gold**: Requires near-perfect racing lines, trail-braking, minimal scrub, and optimum gear selection.
- **Silver**: Solid execution of fundamentals, minor line or braking point imperfections.
- **Bronze**: Safe completion within the standard target window. Minimum required to unlock the next test or license.

## 3. Gran Turismo 2 (PS1, 1999) License Breakdown

Gran Turismo 2 featured **6 licenses** with **10 tests each** (60 tests total). GT2 introduced rally licenses and distinct drivetrain comparisons.

### 3.1 License Structure & Prize Cars

- **National B License**: 10 tests. Gold Reward: _Spoon S2000 (J)_
- **National A License**: 10 tests. Gold Reward: _Dodge Concept Car (Copperhead)_
- **International C License (IC)**: 10 tests. Gold Reward: _Mitsubishi 3000GT LM Edition_
- **International B License (IB)**: 10 tests. Gold Reward: _Honda CR-X Del Sol LM Edition_
- **International A License (IA)**: 10 tests. Gold Reward: _Mitsubishi FTO LM Edition_
- **Super License (S)**: 10 tests. Gold Reward: _Toyota GT-One Race Car (TS020)_

## 4. Gran Turismo 4 (PS2, 2004) License Breakdown

Gran Turismo 4 represents the zenith of the license test formula. It expanded the structure to **5 categories with 16 tests each (80 tests total)** plus **5 "Coffee Break" gymkhana tests**.

### 4.1 License Categories

1. **National B (B-1 to B-16)**: Entry-level dynamics, basic braking, first real track sectors.
2. **National A (A-1 to A-16)**: Complex corner combinations, elevation changes, hairpins, sports cars.
3. **International B (IB-1 to IB-16)**: Race cars, high-speed sectors, advanced chicane cutting, pace car challenges.
4. **International A (IA-1 to IA-16)**: Prototype and GT machinery, wet surfaces, Le Mans chicanes, blind complexes.
5. **Special / Super License (S-1 to S-16)**: Full hot laps across 16 world circuits (including Nürburgring Nordschleife).
6. **Coffee Breaks**: Cones knocking, 360° gymkhana spins, parking box challenges.

---

## 5. Gran Turismo 5 (PS3, 2010) License Breakdown

Gran Turismo 5 modernized the structure with higher-definition physics, telemetric ghost guidance, and dynamic weather/track conditions.

### 5.1 License Categories & Structure

- **National B (B-1 to B-10)**: Basic maneuvers, Top Gear slalom, small city tracks.
- **National A (A-1 to A-10)**: Mid-field tracks, night driving, high-speed cornering.
- **International C (IC-1 to IC-10)**: Sports cars, elevation, wet conditions.
- **International B (IB-1 to IB-10)**: Race cars, blind corners, drafting/slipstreaming.
- **International A (IA-1 to IA-10)**: Super GT, NASCAR, Le Mans prototypes.
- **Super License (S-1 to S-10)**: Complete timed hot laps in high-downforce cars.

## 6. Pedagogical Matrix for Assetto Corsa Adaptation

To translate Gran Turismo's formula into an Assetto Corsa mod, we map the pedagogical goals to specific vehicle classes and track types available in AC:

```
[Level 1: Fundamentals]
  B-1 .. B-2   --> 0-100 & 0-200 Braking Box        (Street Hatchbacks: Abarth 500 / Miata)
  B-3 .. B-6   --> Single Turn Entry & Exit          (Front-Wheel & Rear-Wheel Drive Road Cars)
  B-7 .. B-10  --> Slalom & Basic Chicanes           (Balanced Sports Cars: Toyota GT86, Lotus Elise)

[Level 2: Intermediate]
  A-1 .. A-4   --> Linked Hairpins & Elevation Drops (Laguna Seca Corkscrew, Brands Hatch Graham Hill)
  A-5 .. A-8   --> Oversteer / Understeer Recovery   (BMW M3 E30 / E92, Alfa Romeo 4C)
  A-9 .. A-12  --> Technical Track Sectors           (Silverstone Becketts, Imola Variante Alta)

[Level 3: Advanced & High Downforce]
  IB / IA      --> Aero Downforce & High Speed Lines (Porsche 911 GT3 RS, Ferrari 488 GT3)
  S-License    --> Full Circuit Precision Hot Laps   (Nordschleife, Spa, Monza, Mugello)
```
