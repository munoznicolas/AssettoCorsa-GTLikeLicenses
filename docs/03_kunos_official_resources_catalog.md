# Kunos Official Resources Catalog (Assetto Corsa: Ultimate Edition)

## 1. Overview & DLC Architecture

This document provides a comprehensive inventory of all official content released by **Kunos Simulazioni** for *Assetto Corsa*, encompassing the base simulation, all official downloadable content (DLC) packages, and free bonus updates that comprise the **Assetto Corsa: Ultimate Edition**.

### 1.1 Official Content Packages Included
1. **Base Game Content** (Release v1.0 through v1.16)
2. **Dream Pack 1** (Nürburgring Nordschleife + 10 cars)
3. **Dream Pack 2** (Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya + 9 cars)
4. **Dream Pack 3** (Brands Hatch + 7 cars)
5. **Japanese Pack** (7 Japanese iconic road and race models)
6. **Red Pack** (Red Bull Ring + 7 Italian performance models)
7. **Tripl3 Pack** (Ferrari 488 GTB, FXX K, Praga R1)
8. **Porsche Pack Volume 1** (7 Porsche historic and modern models)
9. **Porsche Pack Volume 2** (9 Porsche road and racing models)
10. **Porsche Pack Volume 3** (7 Porsche GT, LMP1, and historic models)
11. **Ready to Race Pack** (10 diverse race and track-day models)
12. **Ferrari 70th Anniversary Celebration Pack** (7 landmark Ferrari machines)
13. **Free Bonus Packs & Additions**:
    * Bonus Pack 1 (Zandvoort, Alfa Romeo MiTo QV, Audi S1, Nissan GT-R NISMO)
    * Bonus Pack 2 (Black Cat County, Ford Mustang 2015, Corvette C7 Stingray)
    * Bonus Pack 3 (Laguna Seca + 7 performance road cars)
    * Highlands (Scottish road layout variations)
    * Classic historic circuits (Silverstone 1967, Monza 1966)

Total official content count: **19 circuit locations (over 45 unique layout configurations)** and **178 vehicles**.
