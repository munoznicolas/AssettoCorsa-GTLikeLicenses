# Kunos Official Resources Catalog (Assetto Corsa: Ultimate Edition)

## 1. Overview

To guarantee that the **GT-Like Licenses Mod** is accessible to the broadest possible sim racing audience, all core license tiers (National B, National A, International, and Super License) can be constructed **exclusively using official content from Kunos Simulazioni**.

The *Assetto Corsa: Ultimate Edition* package includes the base simulation plus all 11 official DLC releases and free Bonus Packs:
- **Dream Pack 1, 2, and 3**
- **Japanese Pack**
- **Red Pack**
- **Tripl3 Pack**
- **Porsche Pack Vol. 1, Vol. 2, and Vol. 3**
- **Ready to Race Pack**
- **Ferrari 70th Anniversary Celebration Pack**
- **Free Bonus Packs 1, 2, 3, Highlands, and Classic Tracks**

Across these releases, Kunos provides **19 circuit locations (with over 45 laser-scanned layouts)** and **178 official vehicles**, spanning road hatchbacks, analog sports cars, homologation specials, GT3/GTE machinery, Group C legends, LMP1 prototypes, and Formula 1 eras.
